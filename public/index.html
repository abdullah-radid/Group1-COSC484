Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before moving in

How do I apply for an apartment?

You can begin the application process by contacting our leasing office via the inquiry form below or by calling us directly. We'll walk you through availability, pricing, and the application requirements.

What is included in the monthly rent?

Monthly rent includes water, trash, and access to all community amenities including the pool, fitness center, and resident lounge. Electricity and internet are the resident's responsibility.

Do you allow pets?

Yes, we are pet-friendly! Dogs and cats under 40 lbs are welcome with prior written approval. A one-time non-refundable pet deposit of $300 is required per pet.

What lease terms are available?

We offer 6-month and 12-month lease terms. Month-to-month arrangements may be available upon request at an adjusted rate. Please contact the leasing office for current availability.

How do I submit a maintenance request?

Current residents can submit maintenance requests through the Resident Portal. Our maintenance team typically responds within 24–48 hours for non-emergency requests, and immediately for emergencies.

Is parking available?

Yes, one assigned parking space is included with each unit. Additional spaces may be available for a monthly fee. Visitor parking is also available on a first-come, first-served basis.
