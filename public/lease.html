The monthly rent for Unit 205 is $1,200.00, due on the 1st of each month. A grace period of 5 days is provided. Payments received after the 6th of the month will incur a late fee of $75.00.

Only residents listed on the lease agreement may occupy the unit. Guests may stay no longer than 14 consecutive days without prior written approval from management. Subletting is not permitted.

Pets are permitted with prior written approval. A non-refundable pet deposit of $300 is required per pet. Only dogs and cats under 40 lbs are permitted. Exotic animals are not allowed.

Residents are responsible for maintaining the unit in clean condition and promptly reporting any damage or maintenance issues. Management is responsible for structural repairs, plumbing, and HVAC systems.
