FAQ

Quick answers for potential and current residents.

How do I create a resident account?

Use the sign up page and enter the resident name, email, password, unit number, and lease end date.

Can residents submit maintenance requests online?

Yes. After logging in, residents can submit maintenance requests through the resident menu.

Can residents pay rent online?

Yes. Residents can submit rent payments from the resident portal.

Who can view resident statuses?

Admin users can view resident records, rent status, and account information.
